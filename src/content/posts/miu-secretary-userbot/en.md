AI secretary for your personal Telegram account — works as a Telethon userbot, no bot token or Business subscription required. It understands the context of people who message you and replies, delivers important messages to you immediately, and handles the rest itself — never on your behalf, as an open AI secretary. It understands images and voice messages, retains memory, and is managed only via your personal inbox.

## Open-source, free for everyone and can be improve by anyone
